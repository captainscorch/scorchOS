# The Portfolio at Your Fingertips

HÜBNER Group from Kassel develops systems, components and services for rail and road vehicles, from gangway and articulation systems to window and entry solutions. The group shows this portfolio at the two big fairs of its industry: InnoTrans in Berlin, the world's largest trade fair for rail technology, and Busworld Europe in Brussels. Next to the exhibits, the booth runs large touchscreens, and since Busworld Europe 2023 I have been leading the development of the software on them, as a project of my studio unlimited.studio. My business partner Michel and Laura design the screens, and Anton helps me build them.

The interface travels to every fair, the smaller ones included, where it mostly runs on an iPad. That is where it counts most, because there is hardly any room for exhibits.

## Two Apps, One Component System

Neither fair presence is a website. Each one is an app that runs straight on the booth hardware, without a server or an internet connection. I set up Rail and Road on one shared component system: masthead, product boxes, image galleries, videos, fact cards, timeline and navigation exist once and serve both apps, while each fair keeps its own content. A component guide shows every building block with its variants in every screen format, so feedback from HÜBNER lands on exactly the right element.

## From Vehicle to Solution

At the booth, visitors tap their way from the full vehicle into the individual solutions: Front End, Intercar, Bogie, Window, Insulation, Entry, Service and Digital Services. Each product page combines technical facts, renderings, image galleries, videos and QR codes to further information.

- **Navigation:** A fixed bar with Home, Products, What's New, Affiliates and HÜBNER Group. Its flyouts show the full product tree, and breadcrumbs keep visitors oriented on every level.
- **Affiliates:** A page of its own for each company in the group.
- **Highlights:** A page for the new products of each fair.
- **Interactive Content:** Service phases open as a detail view with a tap. At Busworld, visitors rotate and zoom the bus articulation as a 3D model built with three.js.

## One Layout for Every Device

The booth runs portrait steles in 4K and Full HD, 4K landscape displays and desktop and laptop screens. iPad versions came later, so the sales team can work with the apps too. I tuned the layout to exactly this hardware instead of common smartphone sizes. On a 4K landscape display the app renders the approved Full HD layout at exactly twice the size, so every device shows the same design.

## Built for the Fair Floor

- **No Installation:** I deliver a finished build that starts straight from the device in the browser.
- **Access Protection:** A password locks the interface until the fair opens.
- **Touch-Safe:** Context menu, pinch zoom and double-tap zoom are disabled, so visitors can't knock the view out of place.
