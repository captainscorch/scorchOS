# Furniture You Have to See in a Room

cale design designs and builds steel furniture in its own workshop and sells it alongside a curated range of furniture and home accessories, in its showroom and in its [Shopify store](/case-study/cale). A steel coffee table only makes its case once you see it next to a sofa. So I shoot both for cale: the product on white and the furnished room around it.

## Five Shoot Days, One Look

This selection comes from five shoot days between February 2025 and October 2026.

- **Showroom, February 2025:** Product shots of lamps and vases on the shelves, and a few days later the showroom itself at its opening.
- **Workshop Launch, June 2025:** Tables in pink, black and white and the kids wardrobe MILLE in front of mustard and grey curtains, plus cutouts.
- **Shop the Room, March 2026:** Furnished rooms in the studio of the artist ninibing, with a fireplace, plants and everyday scenes. Room 01 and Room 04 in the shop come from this day.
- **Coffee Tables, October 2026:** Coffee tables in three colours in a lived-in living room instead of a studio.

## From Photo to Cart

The photos do not end up in a press kit. They end up where people buy.

- **Shop the Room:** The room photos become collections with product hotspots. Tap the table in the picture and you land on the table.
- **Theme Tiles:** The homepage shows the product worlds as photo tiles from the shoots.
- **Cutouts:** Products on white for the shop, shot in the light box.

## Colors That Were Missing

Not every color was ready on shoot day. I recolored the black table from the manufacture launch in post-production from another finish, so the shop could show every variant. It only stood in front of the camera itself at the autumn 2026 shoot.

## Post-Production

I edit every photo in Lightroom Classic. The photos come from two cameras, different years and very different rooms, from the showroom to a living room. In the shop they still have to sit next to each other as if they came from one day.

A table sells once you can picture it at home.
